# Milestone desk

## Scope and visitor mode

Primary target: web/index.html and the root workspace route. Mode: Operate.

## Audience and job

A student freelancer and client review a short project through explicitly labeled local demo roles. Select a project, read its accepted scope, submit or inspect evidence, record the permitted decision, and inspect the corresponding local test-token receipt.

## Direction and memorable moment

The delivery tracking desk joins a numbered work sequence to an adjacent agreement and receipt history. The useful moment is accepting a delivered milestone and seeing its confirmed local transaction retained with the decision.

## Composition and responsive behavior

A shallow navy identity header and project toolbar lead into the selected project. Desktop places a broad milestone sheet beside a narrower agreement sheet, with receipts below. At 761–900px the toolbar wraps to two rows. At 760px and below, Project and Demo role stay side by side, connection/create controls have their own row, and the agreement precedes milestones. View milestones gives a direct route to work. Agreement options groups secondary actions in a closed disclosure.

## Proof and constraints

Use actual persisted project data and Anvil receipts. The selected role determines the next permitted action. Local demo labels, token units and settlement limitations remain explicit. Public testnet deployment and production authentication are not capabilities of this surface. Evidence, scope fingerprints and transaction hashes are readable details rather than decorative proof labels.

The supplied finish verdict is ship, with all three material findings resolved. No unresolved visual decision remains in this implementation. Public testnet and production account work are separate future product work, not a visual promise.
