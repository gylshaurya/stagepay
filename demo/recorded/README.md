# Recorded demo

The captioned demo is 131.76 seconds, with no narration. It shows real browser actions against local contracts, followed by a separate read-only agreement and real receipts on the public Sepolia site. Test tokens are labelled throughout.

The local take created agreement 3, requested a revision without payment, accepted the corrected setup guide, agreed a scope change and withdrew 10 DEMO. Nine local transaction receipts were verified. The remaining 20 DEMO stays in local escrow for the unfinished guide. The separate public agreement 1 completed settlement and both withdrawals, with zero remaining escrow or wallet credit.

The recorder captured actual selected-browser tab frames at about 4.9 frames per second, encoded to a 30 fps H.264 MP4. Idle gaps between scenes were trimmed; actions were not sped up. Captions sit below the product viewport. One public-ending outtake was retained privately and replaced after a tab was closed.

All 27 captions are under 17 characters per second; the measured peak is 16.54. Early, middle and public-ending frames were inspected. Full MP4 decoding passed. Full browser playback reached the end without a media error.
